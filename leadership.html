About Anthony T. Stevenson

Anthony T. Stevenson brings over 20 years of distinguished experience in corporate advisory, government consulting, and enterprise systems implementation. As President and Chief Executive Officer of Accounting & Computer Solutions, Inc. (ACS), he provides the strategic vision that guides our services for complex federal and commercial clientele.

Throughout his career, Mr. Stevenson has specialized in high-stakes financial systems modernization, ERP implementations, and structured IT strategic alignment. His expertise bridges the gap between sophisticated accounting protocols and complex technical architectures, establishing highly compliant, scalable frameworks designed to pass rigorous government audits and meet unique program objectives.

Under his active leadership, ACS has evolved into a trusted mission partner, modernizing enterprise technology footprints for prime defense agencies, public sector organizations, and growing commercial entities.
